# ADR-0001: Record architecture decisions

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-06

## Context

Design choices in a streaming system are easy to forget and expensive to rediscover.

## Decision

Every significant decision is recorded as a short ADR in `docs/adr/`, numbered in order,
with context, decision, and consequences. Superseded ADRs stay in the repo, marked
`Superseded by ADR-NNNN`.

## Consequences

Reviewers can see why, not just what. The cost is a few minutes per decision.
